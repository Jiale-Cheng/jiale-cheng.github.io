## About This Site

I started this site to share notes from topics I was learning. It now also serves as an academic homepage while preserving older writing in information theory, communications, mathematics, and related areas. Some older notes are in Chinese and may reflect an earlier stage of my studies.

Posts use the following status labels:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Completed | The post is substantially complete, though small revisions may still appear. |
| Writing | The post is actively being developed. |
| Paused | Work is temporarily paused. |
| Archived | The post is retained for reference but is not expected to receive substantial updates. |

The [source code](https://github.com/Jiale-Cheng/jiale-cheng.github.io) is publicly available. Licensing information and third-party acknowledgements are recorded in the repository's [license file](https://github.com/Jiale-Cheng/jiale-cheng.github.io/blob/main/LICENSE.md).
